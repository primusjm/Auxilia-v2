## Suggested candidates for the band

The band has room for four numbers in one row. These are drawn from the tables below; nothing here has been added to the site.

**Already in the band (all measured, all linked to a client):** +38% programme enquiries (RLC), +61% online gifts (Inuka), 1M+ trees planted (Kenya Power Foundation), +27% scholarship applicants (Co-op Bank Foundation).

**Strong alternatives or additions:**
- **2.4×** traffic to funded-research pages (Science for Africa Foundation). A measured result, and the only one not yet in the band.
- **1,900** applications in cycle one (RSIF · PASET). An outcome count.
- **KSh 39M** collected towards a KSh 50M goal (Inuka). A strong number, but the label needs the goal to read fairly.
- **130+** leaders from 14 organisations (RLC), **11,000+** students supported (Co-op Bank Foundation) and **10.1M+** customers served (Kenya Power). These show reach, but they describe the client’s scale, not a change we made.

**Studies with no measured result yet:** GRiC, Promises, AHFIA and Imara only have descriptive numbers. They could join the band only if Josh has real outcome figures for them.

**Keep apart:** the appendix numbers (50+ years, 460bn+ KES, 306,000 km of network, and so on) are the clients’ own facts. They suit the appendix section, not a band of Auxilia’s results.
